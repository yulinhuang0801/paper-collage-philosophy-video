# Paper Collage Philosophy Video Production

A production skill for making bilingual paper collage philosophy explainer videos, proven on episode 1, "The Chinese Room" (John Searle).

- `SKILL.md` holds the method: content rules, script shape, storyboard grammar, image and clip production, timing re-anchoring to recorded narration, the data driven shot build, render QA, and platform publishing.
- `references/` holds the episode 1 records the method was distilled from: the full script, the narration segment data, the v3 storyboard, and the Chinese and English shot timing tables.
- `scripts/` holds the builders used in production, including the bilingual shot mode renderer `build_v3.py`.

Episode 1: [Could a Machine Understand You? — The Chinese Room](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_cFOyDM56DM)
